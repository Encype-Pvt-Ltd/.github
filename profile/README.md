# Encype

We are a senior team that transforms business challenges into effective software solutions. We apply AI where it adds value and rely on robust engineering elsewhere, delivering systems your team can operate independently.

### What we do

- We automate manual processes, including document-to-data extraction, compliance workflows, and scheduled reporting.
- We develop production-ready full-stack applications, from initial architecture to launch, ensuring your team can maintain them.
- We ensure reliable operations through infrastructure-as-code on AWS with Terraform, automated deployments, and resilient systems.

### Overview of Our Project

| Product | Status | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| [ScanLinQ](https://github.com/ScanLinQ) | Early access | AI gateway and workflow builder offering a unified API for multiple models, with integrated failover and BYOK encryption. |
| [Prorganizer Space](https://prorganizer.space/) | Shipped | We offer finance, compliance, and payroll solutions for Indian IT exporters, with AI-powered invoice extraction and automated deadline reminders. |
| Ecombiz AI | In development | We provide order management, inventory synchronisation, and shipment tracking across Amazon, eBay, Walmart, and Shopify. |
| EdTech platform | Shipped | We offer real-time AI chat, intelligent agents, content management, and universal search to support your business needs. |

### Technology Stack

We use Scala (Cats, Play, Akka), TypeScript (React, Next.js, Express), Python, and Rust, deployed on AWS with Terraform.
